I am a PhD student in Informatics at King’s College London, supervised by Dr. Yali Du. I study how multiple agents, whether robots or AI models, can coordinate to solve hard problems reliably and efficiently. My current PhD work focuses on cooperative aerial transport with multiple drones, and I am also interested in reinforcement learning and in how to combine large language models (LLMs) under a cost budget.

Before KCL, I received an M.Eng. in Artificial Intelligence from Beijing Institute of Technology and a B.Eng. in Artificial Intelligence from Shandong University. My published work covers energy-aware coverage planning for single and multiple UAVs over mountainous terrain.

02 / Publications

Selected work

2026IEEE T-ITS

Energy-Aware Multi-UAV Coverage Routing Over Mountainous Terrain via Contour-Aligned Primitives and Clustering-Based Fleet Allocation

Wenbin Xu, Qi Shao, Xiangdong Liu, Zhi Wang, Ying Feng, Xuefei Mao, and Zhen Li

IEEE Transactions on Intelligent Transportation Systems, 2026

Groups terrain-following path segments into balanced clusters, one per UAV, then routes each UAV through its cluster, balancing total energy against the load on the busiest UAV.

2025IEEE RA-L

Energy-Aware UAV Coverage Planning in Mountainous Terrain via Contour-Aligned Path Generation

Qi Shao, Xuefei Mao, and Wenbin Xu

IEEE Robotics and Automation Letters, vol. 10, no. 12, pp. 12373–12380, 2025

Builds coverage paths along terrain contour lines and orders them with an energy-aware routing solver. On 20 mountainous maps it reduced mission energy by about 35% compared with grid-based planning while keeping about 98% coverage.
